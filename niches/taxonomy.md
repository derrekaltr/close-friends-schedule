# Creator niche taxonomy — the content-lane layer for Close Friends

Ported from the `modelsniche` research project (21 niches verified against live Instagram profiles on 2026-08-26; example handles and the tagging/classifier tooling live there, not here). This copy is distilled for one job: **once you know a creator's niche, you know which Close Friends content lane to generate for.**

How to use with this project: resolve the creator's niche with [`decision-guide.md`](decision-guide.md) (or take it from the roster), then pull that niche's **voice**, **visual signature** and **CF content angles** below — or programmatically from [`niches.json`](niches.json) — as the conditioning for story ideas. Most creators are a **hybrid** (base + differentiator); blend the two lanes, lead with the differentiator.

Close Friends ground rules that apply to every niche: CF is the *intimate* tier — low production beats polished, daily beats perfect, direct address ("you") beats broadcast, and every third story should pull a reply (poll, question box, "DM me X"). The niche decides the *flavor*, not these mechanics.

## Family A — Aesthetic-led

### A1 · Girl-next-door / natural-relatable
- **One-liner:** "A real girl you could actually meet" — attainability is the product.
- **Fit for new creators:** High — cheapest to execute, but saturated; works best as the *base* under a differentiator.
- **Voice:** chatty, self-deprecating, ♡ and 🥺, soft coded CTAs.
- **Visual:** natural light, phone selfies, bedroom/kitchen/car, minimal retouching.
- **Audience:** broad male 20–45 buying accessibility and responsiveness.
- **Hybrids:** any trait or lifestyle niche; GFE as the DM layer.
- **CF angles:** just-woke-up bedhead selfie · "help me pick" outfit poll · day-in-my-life micro-vlog · question-box AMA night · "only you guys see this" home-routine tease.

### A2 · Glamour / bombshell
- **One-liner:** polished, editorial, aspirational — the feed is the magazine cover, CF is the outtakes.
- **Fit:** Low–Medium — needs shoot budget and a model look.
- **Voice:** confident, short captions, aspirational.
- **Visual:** studio/luxury settings, pro lighting, lingerie/designer styling, glossy grid.
- **Audience:** buyers of production value.
- **Hybrids:** pin-up, fitness, country ("yeehaw glam").
- **CF angles:** unretouched shoot outtakes "CF only" · getting-ready glam sequence · hotel-room outfit reveal · "which look tonight" poll · behind-the-set chaos clips.

### A3 · Alt / goth / tattooed (punk, emo, grunge sub-variants)
- **One-liner:** dark, modded, visually unmistakable — every frame reads as goth without a caption.
- **Fit:** High if it's real — the niche punishes tourists; mods are unfakeable authenticity.
- **Voice:** deadpan or playful-dark, 🖤.
- **Visual:** black/deep-red/purple, tattoos foregrounded, corsetry, platforms, lace/latex.
- **Audience:** alt-identified men and women; low competition, high search volume.
- **Hybrids:** cosplay/e-girl, MILF ("goth mom"), femdom-coded.
- **CF angles:** outfit/corset build-up piece by piece · fresh-ink close-up reveal · makeup transformation timelapse · "band tee or latex tonight" poll · chaotic 3am energy dumps.

### A4 · Bimbo / Barbie hyper-feminine
- **One-liner:** self-aware maximal femininity — pink, plastic, proud.
- **Fit:** Medium — persona must be total; cosmetic investment is real; instantly legible brand.
- **Voice:** bubbly, 💕🎀💋, "princess treatment" framing.
- **Visual:** pink-dominant, big blonde hair, gloss, doll/princess props.
- **Audience:** bimbofication-adjacent plus mainstream glamour buyers.
- **Hybrids:** glamour, musician, bratty-domme.
- **CF angles:** pink haul try-on · wishlist "spoil me" tease · exaggerated GRWM · "rate me 1–10" mirror check · princess-treatment storytime.

### A5 · Pin-up / retro / vintage
- **One-liner:** 1940s–60s glamour codes — red lip, victory rolls, gingham — "tasteful" is the sell.
- **Fit:** Medium — styling skill required; very low competition; ages well.
- **Voice:** playful-classy.
- **Visual:** warm/sepia grading, period wardrobe, burlesque props, classic cars.
- **Audience:** older, higher-spend collectors; burlesque/cosplay fandom overlap.
- **Hybrids:** glamour, cosplay, MILF.
- **CF angles:** victory-roll GRWM · vintage-lingerie detail pans · polaroid-style still drops · "name this set" question box · retro homemaking interlude (baking in the look).

## Family B — Persona / role-led

### B1 · Girlfriend experience (GFE)
- **One-liner:** "your online girlfriend" — the product is the relationship, not the photo. **This is the default CF operating model for every niche.**
- **Fit:** High (skill-gated) — zero production cost; the constraint is DM stamina and emotional consistency.
- **Voice:** second person, affectionate, continuous.
- **Visual:** deliberately intimate and low-production — the grid looks like a partner's camera roll.
- **Audience:** men paying for attention and continuity; retention-driven.
- **Hybrids:** layers onto everything.
- **CF angles:** "good morning baby" direct address · voice-note replies to DMs · mid-day "thinking of you" selfie · goodnight check-in · "if you were here right now…" POV · reply-bait ("first 10 DMs get…").

### B2 · Profession persona ("uniform niche")
- **One-liner:** a real (or plausibly real) job as the hook — the secret-double-life narrative is the marketing.
- **Fit:** Medium — needs a credible job story; real employment risk.
- **Voice:** double-entendre on the job.
- **Visual:** uniform/workwear props, hotel layovers, office settings.
- **Audience:** fantasy-of-access buyers; high press virality.
- **Hybrids:** GND, MILF, college-coded.
- **CF angles:** "just off shift" half-out-of-uniform tease · "guess what happened at work" storytime · layover hotel check-in · countdown sticker "uniform comes off at 8" · locker-room-mirror fit check.

### B3 · Femdom / findom / kink-coded
- **One-liner:** power dynamic as brand — "Goddess", tribute language — explicit layer kept off IG. Instagram is this niche's *weakest* channel (it lives on X/Telegram); CF works only for already-committed subs.
- **Fit:** Low on Instagram.
- **Voice:** imperative, teasing contempt, 👑💸.
- **Visual:** latex/leather, black-red-purple, throne/crown motifs — or bratty e-girl variant.
- **Audience:** small counts, highest per-fan spend.
- **Hybrids:** streamer, goth, bimbo-brat.
- **CF angles:** task-of-the-day post · tribute-leaderboard shoutouts · timed "you may look" drops · boot/latex detail close-ups · degradation Q&A box.

### B4 · Comedy / personality-first
- **One-liner:** funny first, hot second — the paid page is an extension of a comedic persona.
- **Fit:** Medium — requires a genuine comedic voice; un-copyable once it lands.
- **Voice:** the voice IS the brand; no aesthetic uniformity needed.
- **Visual:** chaotic selfies, stage clips, memes.
- **Audience:** existing comedy fans, mixed gender.
- **Hybrids:** GFE ("funny gf"), MILF, musician.
- **CF angles:** unhinged hot-take of the day · thirst-trap-then-punchline bait & switch · crowd-work question box · multi-part storytime cliffhangers · meme commentary in her voice.

## Family C — Lifestyle-led

### C1 · Southern / country / cowgirl
- **One-liner:** boots, trucks, barns and rodeo — American rural identity as the brand.
- **Fit:** High if authentic — location and lifestyle can't be faked; very strong conversion.
- **Voice:** 🤠🥵, down-home, "TOP %" swagger.
- **Visual:** denim, boots, hats, sundresses; golden-hour fields, tailgates, rodeo.
- **Audience:** US male 25–50.
- **Hybrids:** fitness, MILF ("country mama"), glam, trad-wife flavor.
- **CF angles:** golden-hour truck-bed clip · boots-and-sundress GRWM · ranch chores in cutoffs · "yeehaw or nah" outfit poll · bonfire-night story chain.

### C2 · Stoner / 420
- **One-liner:** cannabis lifestyle — glass, smoke, munchies — with the paid page behind the link.
- **Fit:** Medium — platform-restricted content; backup account mandatory; loyal community.
- **Voice:** chill, deadpan, 🍃💨.
- **Visual:** green/haze palette, glass and trays, tie-dye/thrift, lo-fi bedroom light.
- **Audience:** cannabis community, both genders.
- **Hybrids:** GND, alt, "stoner brat".
- **CF angles:** wake-and-bake check-in · new-glass show-and-tell · smoke-trick clip · munchies-run poll · real-time "sesh with me" story chain.

### C3 · Outdoorsy / nature / van-life
- **One-liner:** landscapes, camping, yoga on rocks — free-spirit sensuality.
- **Fit:** Medium — needs location access; very low competition; doubles as a shoot portfolio.
- **Voice:** earthy, unhurried, "freedom" framing.
- **Visual:** earth tones, golden hour, desert/forest, film-look grading.
- **Audience:** art-nude and nature-photo buyers; older, higher spend.
- **Hybrids:** wellness/yoga, pin-up, beach.
- **CF angles:** trailhead-sunrise check-in · campsite fit check · skinny-dip *implication* tease · "where should I shoot next" poll · tent/van goodnight story.

### C4 · Beach / surf / bikini
- **One-liner:** endless-summer bikini content where the "VIP" is the point.
- **Fit:** Medium — easy to produce, hard to differentiate; pair with a trait or persona.
- **Voice:** sunny, 😜💋👙.
- **Visual:** sun-bleached warmth, sand/boat/pool, micro-bikinis, wet hair.
- **Audience:** broad male; crowded with non-adult bikini influencers.
- **Hybrids:** redhead/trait, cosplay, fitness.
- **CF angles:** bikini try-on haul · tan-line reveal tease · beach-day POV · "which bikini" poll · sunset balcony outfit drop.

## Family D — Body / trait-led

### D1 · MILF / mature (35+)
- **One-liner:** age and motherhood ARE the brand — confident, unapologetic.
- **Fit:** High for 35+ — authenticity is the moat; low competition, premium pricing.
- **Voice:** direct, warm, zero apology.
- **Visual:** polished consistent grid, home and everyday settings, presence over filters.
- **Audience:** men 25–60.
- **Hybrids:** comedy, country ("country mama"), goth ("goth mom"), muscle-mommy fitness.
- **CF angles:** morning-coffee robe selfie · "kids are at school…" tease · errands-but-make-it-hot · wine-night Q&A · direct-address confidence pieces.

### D2 · Trait-as-brand (redhead/ginger; height; freckles)
- **One-liner:** one physical trait, named in the handle, repeated everywhere.
- **Fit:** High as a *modifier*, low as a whole identity — cheap durable differentiation if the trait is real.
- **Voice/Visual:** inherits the host niche; palette chosen to flatter the trait; "all-natural / certified" claims.
- **Audience:** search-driven trait devotees (redhead is the best-evidenced).
- **Hybrids:** apply to any host niche.
- **CF angles:** trait-forward close-ups (hair/freckles/height comparisons) · "certified ginger" running jokes · trait-styling before/after · trait-based polls ("up or down today").

### D3 · Fitness-forward
- **One-liner:** gym/athlete content **with** an adult funnel — visually identical to fitness influencers, structurally different.
- **Fit:** Medium — needs a genuine physique and training habit; usually runs a clean main + spicy alt.
- **Voice:** training talk that slides into tease.
- **Visual:** gym mirror, physique checks, meal prep, competition/bikini content.
- **Audience:** physique buyers converting from fitness following.
- **Hybrids:** country, muscle-mommy MILF, male gym-bro, gymnast/athlete persona.
- **CF angles:** gym-fit check · sweaty post-workout selfie · PR clip → "recovery" tease · meal-prep interlude · alt-account "exclusive" pointer drops.

## Family E — Demographic-led

### E1 · Trans creators
- **One-liner:** identity stated up front; the social profile is the authenticity layer fans check before subscribing.
- **Fit:** Medium–High — lower competition, highest LTV in agency data; higher moderation/harassment load.
- **Voice:** direct, community-warm.
- **Visual:** inherits a host niche (fitness, cosplay/gamer, GND); face-forward consistency matters more than for cis niches.
- **Hybrids:** fitness, cosplay/gamer, profession persona.
- **CF angles:** daily authenticity check-ins · GRWM transformation pieces · gaming/cosplay crossover · "ask me anything about me" box · hype-community shoutouts.

### E2 · Male creators (gym-bro, twink, himbo, performer)
- **One-liner:** male adult brand carried by physique, a viral persona, or an explicit industry affiliation.
- **Fit:** Low–Medium — smaller market, predominantly gay-male audience; needs a standout body or hook.
- **Voice:** puns and in-jokes; confident POV.
- **Visual:** shirtless gym (bro), slim editorial (twink), viral-clip energy (himbo), agency polish (performer).
- **Hybrids:** fitness, streamer, couples.
- **CF angles:** shirtless morning routine · pump check after training · running in-joke props · travel/casting storytime · audience-targeted POV direct address.

## Family F — Platform-crossover

### F1 · E-girl / cosplay / gamer
- **One-liner:** anime, gaming and costume craft as identity; conventions as proof-of-work.
- **Fit:** Medium — craft and cost are real; the community pre-exists and rewards skill.
- **Voice:** ":3", waifu-speak, community in-jokes.
- **Visual:** pastel/neon or dark-alt, wigs and builds, RGB rooms, SFX makeup.
- **Audience:** gaming/anime fandom; e-girl × cosplay is the highest-leverage stack.
- **Hybrids:** alt/goth, streamer, trans, "your anime gf" GFE.
- **CF angles:** WIP costume build · character try-on transition · con-prep chaos · "which character next" poll · in-character voice clips.

### F2 · Streamer crossover
- **One-liner:** the only niche where the audience exists before the paid page — CF is the "chat can't see this" tier.
- **Fit:** Low unless already streaming.
- **Voice:** community-insider ("chat", emote-speak).
- **Visual:** stream-setup aesthetics carried into daily life.
- **Hybrids:** e-girl/cosplay, findom, musician.
- **CF angles:** off-stream decompress selfie · "chat can't see this" framing · RGB-room night check-in · reacting to her own clips · post-stream adrenaline dumps.

### F3 · Musician / DJ / artist crossover
- **One-liner:** a real music identity with the paid page as "exclusive access" — rarely works bottom-up.
- **Fit:** Low — requires an actual music career.
- **Voice:** artist-first, scene-coded.
- **Visual:** stage/festival/studio, rave styling.
- **Hybrids:** bimbo/Barbie, streamer, comedy.
- **CF angles:** studio-session sneak peek · unreleased-track snippet "CF ears only" · backstage fit check · "pick the cover art" poll · afterparty story chain.
