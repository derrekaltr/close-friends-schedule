# Post-format library — the shapes a CF story can take

Distilled from real example stories (screenshots reviewed 2026-09-15; kept out of this public repo). The niche layer ([`taxonomy.md`](taxonomy.md)) decides the *flavor*; these formats decide the *shape*. The content engine should pick a format first, then fill it with the creator's niche voice and `cf_angles`.

**The shared DNA of every example:** shot on a phone, single subject, zero production, present-tense "you're in my day right now" energy, frequently **no face in frame**, and humor doing more work than thirst. Close Friends earns the tease by being a real feed the rest of the time.

---

## F01 · Slice-of-life clip
Short vertical clip of an ordinary domestic moment — cooking, coffee, errands — hands and surfaces, face optional. Two-word lowercase caption + one emoji ("taco tuesday 🫶").
- **Why it works:** proof of a real life; the intimacy is the access, not the skin.
- **Recipe:** 5–15s clip · lowercase caption ≤4 words · no sticker needed.
- **Niche flavor:** country → cast-iron on the stove; stoner → munchies run; MILF → school-run coffee; pin-up → baking in the look.

## F02 · Handwritten / analog text post
A whiteboard, fridge note, mirror-marker or napkin message photographed — motivational, funny, or teasing ("I work too hard to not own a boat").
- **Why it works:** text posts feel personal when they're physical objects, not typed overlays.
- **Recipe:** photo of the note · no caption, the note IS the caption · optional ❤️ self-react energy.
- **Niche flavor:** GFE → "good morning, you" note; femdom → task of the day on the board; comedy → punchline of the day; bimbo → lipstick on the mirror.

## F03 · Meme / screenshot repost
A text-message meme or screenshot with a one-line laugh ("You talking to anyone?" — "God"), reposted with a credit tag.
- **Why it works:** personality broadcast; zero effort; invites replies and reshares.
- **Recipe:** meme image centered on plain background · credit tag small · post in her voice (the meme must sound like *her* niche).
- **Niche flavor:** comedy → daily; alt → dark-humor variants; GFE → relationship memes ("me waiting for you to text back").

## F04 · Aesthetic B-roll
A no-face scene-setter: golden-hour street, field from the truck, waves, con floor. Establishes the lifestyle the niche claims.
- **Why it works:** keeps the world real between selfies; costs nothing; feels unguarded.
- **Recipe:** one photo or slow pan · no caption or a location word · best as the first story of the day.
- **Niche flavor:** country → field/ranch; beach → shoreline; outdoorsy → trailhead; musician → venue before doors; streamer → RGB room at night.

## F05 · Recurring prop / in-joke object
A physical object that becomes a running character — a figurine, a named plant, the truck, the bong with a name. Reappears weekly.
- **Why it works:** in-jokes convert followers into insiders; insiders reply.
- **Recipe:** introduce the object once with a name · recur weekly in new situations · let fans ask about it.
- **Niche flavor:** stoner → desk ninja holding the blunt; cosplay → the wig head; gym → the lucky shaker; GND → the cat.

## F06 · Behind-the-scenes operator flex
The unglamorous machinery of her actual work — shoot-day chaos, editing desk, the setup, feet up mid-grind.
- **Why it works:** "you see what nobody else sees" is the whole CF promise, applied to work instead of skin.
- **Recipe:** wide shot of the workspace/setup · deadpan caption ("another day") · no explanation.
- **Niche flavor:** glamour → set BTS and outtake energy; streamer → off-stream desk; musician → studio session; fitness → 5am gym opens.

## F07 · Edgy event / candid spice
The spicy-adjacent candid: pole class, shoot afterparty, outfit that almost breaks the rules — shot like someone's camera roll, not like content.
- **Why it works:** the tease lands harder when it looks accidental and everything around it was real life.
- **Recipe:** candid framing, imperfect light · no caption or a single emoji · never more than one per day.
- **Niche flavor:** each niche's `cf_angles` teases slot here — this is where taxonomy angles like "tan-line reveal" or "corset build-up" live.

## The weekly mix

The examples skew heavily non-thirst — that's the lesson. A CF week that converts reads like a life with occasional lightning:

| Share | Formats |
|---|---|
| ~35% | F01 slice-of-life |
| ~20% | F03 memes + F02 analog text |
| ~15% | F04 aesthetic B-roll |
| ~15% | F07 candid spice (the niche `cf_angles` teases) |
| ~10% | F06 BTS flex + F05 prop recurrences |
| ~5% | direct interactive (polls/question boxes — attach to any format above) |

Rules of thumb: open the day with F04 or F01, never with F07 · one F07 max per day · every third story carries a reply mechanic · F05 recurs on a fixed weekday so insiders expect it.
